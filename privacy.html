Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The Inner Edge provides an AI-assisted content creation workspace for developing educational short-form videos. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the website and its optional TikTok integration.

1. Information We Handle

When you use the content studio, information you enter into the workspace may include topics, scripts, captions, production instructions and generated content. Some creative project data is stored locally in your browser.

If you connect TikTok, The Inner Edge receives the authorization information and tokens necessary to act on your behalf through TikTok's Content Posting API. These credentials are handled by the website's server-side API and are not displayed in the website interface.

2. TikTok Data

The TikTok integration uses the video.publish permission to obtain creator posting settings and, when you explicitly choose to post, send the selected video and posting metadata to TikTok. Creator information such as username, available privacy options, and interaction settings may be retrieved to display the posting controls required by TikTok.

The Inner Edge does not ask for or store your TikTok password. TikTok authentication and authorization are handled by TikTok. You can revoke access through TikTok's account and app authorization controls.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide the requested content-generation, production and publishing features, maintain the service, respond to support requests, and improve the user experience.

4. Third-Party Services

The Inner Edge may use third-party services including Google Gemini, TikTok, Vercel, GitHub and licensed media providers. Those services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Cookies and Security

The website may use essential, HttpOnly, secure cookies for authentication state and authorized TikTok session credentials. These cookies are used to protect the authorization flow and allow the server to call TikTok on your behalf.

6. Data Retention

Local creative projects remain in your browser until you remove them or clear the site's browser storage. TikTok authorization credentials are retained only as needed to maintain the authorized connection and publishing workflow, subject to the token validity periods and service configuration.

7. Children's Privacy

The Inner Edge is a general-audience service and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information made available by The Inner Edge.
