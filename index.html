FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is The Inner Edge?

The Inner Edge is an educational media brand exploring psychology, self-improvement, Stoicism, discipline, money, AI, technology, productivity and human behavior.

Is the content financial or medical advice?

No. The content is intended for general educational and informational purposes and should not replace professional medical, financial, legal or other professional advice.

Does The Inner Edge use AI?

AI may be used as a production and research tool. Human review remains part of the intended content workflow.

Can creators publish to TikTok?

Where an approved TikTok integration is available, authorized users may use supported Content Posting API workflows. Availability depends on TikTok approval, authorization and applicable platform requirements.

Is The Inner Edge affiliated with TikTok?

No. The Inner Edge is an independent brand and is not owned, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.
