Title
Your Brain Is Addicted to Distraction
Hook
Your brain may be fighting your productivity without you realizing it.
Script
Most people think distraction is simply a discipline problem. But your brain is constantly being offered something new. A notification. A message. A new video. A new headline. Novelty gives your attention a reason to move. The problem isn't that your brain is broken. The problem is that your environment is competing for your attention every second. So instead of asking, "How do I become more disciplined?" Start asking: "What am I allowing to interrupt me?" Protect your attention, and you protect your ability to think.
Caption
Your attention is being competed for every second of the day. The first step isn't more motivation. It's fewer interruptions.
